## Why it's written this way

- **The first ~125 characters show before "more".** They hold the hook (641 bales, a real number from the sheet) and the brands buyers search for: Levi's, Lee & Wrangler, Carhartt.
- **The caption is what Instagram search reads**, not the slides, so it names the main brands and bale types. Every one is in the October sheet.
- **The caption carries what the slides dropped.** The client's [July carousel](../../../docs/research/july-carousel.md) put the full address on every page and ended with a "Can't find what you're looking for?" slide. Ours keeps the slides to the list, so the street address and the "ask for a category" line live here instead.
- **One call to action:** WhatsApp, with what to send (bale names and quantities) so the first message is an order, not "price?". The number is written out because tapping an image does nothing. LINE stays off, as on the slides.
- **5 hashtags**, the skill's limit for Instagram. The last one is Thai ("wholesale second-hand clothes") for local resellers.
- **No unconfirmed claims.** July said "worldwide shipping", "fresh stock every month" and "bulk orders welcome". Those wait for the client ([open question 13](../../../docs/client/open-questions.md)). It also doesn't say the stock has arrived, because "ETA141026" may mean it lands on 14 October ([open question 12](../../../docs/client/open-questions.md)).

## Add once the client confirms

- **Question 13:** if confirmed, add a line after the second paragraph, for example "Bulk orders welcome. We ship worldwide."
- **Question 12:** if the stock lands on 14 October, change the hook's start to "October Stock Drop, landing 14 October:".

## Optional

- **Thai line:** add one Thai sentence for local resellers, written or checked by the client's Thai presenter.
- **Alt text:** in Instagram's advanced settings, give each slide alt text listing its sections, for example "Retro Revival Stock Drop October 2026: Jeans". It helps search and screen readers.
