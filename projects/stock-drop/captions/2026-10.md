## Why it's written this way

- **The first ~125 characters show before "more".** They hold the hook and the brands buyers search for: Levi's, Carhartt, Dickies.
- **The caption is what Instagram search reads**, not the slides. That's why it names the main brands and bale types.
- **One call to action:** message on WhatsApp or LINE. The numbers are written out because tapping an image does nothing.
- **5 hashtags**, the skill's limit for Instagram. The last one is Thai ("wholesale second-hand clothes") for local resellers.
- **No invented claims.** Every brand and category comes from the October sheet. It doesn't say the stock has arrived, because "ETA141026" may mean it arrives on 14 October.
