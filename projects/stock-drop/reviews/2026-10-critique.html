Critique · 5-dimension review

Stock Drop, October 2026

A clear step up. The cover now shows what’s inside, and the list reads cleanly. Small list text is the main thing keeping it from premium.

Up from 6.4 (first review, same day). Fixed since: the cover title collision, the unreadable footer logo, the line clutter, and the repeated “BALES” labels.

7.2/10Average of five scores (second review). Reviewed: 7 slides at 1080 × 1350, from projects/stock-drop/output/2026-10/.

Direction

8/10Strong

Consistent throughout. The gold line drawing of bales picks up the logo’s gold. The cover pills and the list containers share one soft, rounded shape, and slides 02–07 use a single template. One drift: the logo plate is the only element with a hard shadow and a framed rule, while everything else is flat and soft.

  • Keep One shape language: pills on the cover, containers on the list.
  • Fix Soften the logo plate’s shadow to match the flat style.

Hierarchy

8/10Strong

Each list slide reads in order: section name (54px serif), then the name-and-packing container, then the gold bale-count box. The single gold “BALES” label above the boxes makes the numbers unambiguous without repeating on every section. The cover reads “Stock Drop”, then the month, then the pills. The 17 pills are many, but they stay quiet.

  • Keep Name → packing → bale-count box.

Detail

7/10Strong

Clean: containers and boxes share a 12px radius, every bale-count box is the same 104px wide with centred figures, and the title is checked against overflow. An expert eye still finds misses. On the cover, about 200px of empty space separates the drawing from the title, so the top and bottom halves feel disconnected. The drawing’s top sits about 45px below the logo plate’s top, so they don’t share a top line. Slide 07 ends with its lower half empty.

  • Keep Equal box widths and radii.
  • Fix Align the drawing’s top with the logo plate.
  • Quick win Close the gap above the title.

Function

6/10Functional

Better at its job. The cover now says what’s inside, the bale counts are easy to find, and contacts are on every slide. One problem remains on all six list slides: at --row: 54px the bale names render at about 24px on a 1080px slide, under the social skill’s ~28pt minimum for phones, and packing is 17px. Every slide has this, so under the worst-band rule it sets the score.

  • Keep Contacts on every slide; categories on the cover.
  • Fix Decide text size: 7 slides at 24px, or 8–9 with larger rows.

Originality

7/10Strong

One memorable move, and it fits: the gold line drawing of strapped bales, with layers and a label tag. It shows the actual product, so it feels part of the brand, not stuck on. The separate bale-count boxes are also a little unusual for a stock list. The rest is suitably restrained.

  • Keep The bale drawing.
