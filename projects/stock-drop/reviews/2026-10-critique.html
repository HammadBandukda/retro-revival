Critique · 5-dimension review

Stock Drop, October 2026

Strong and phone-ready. The remaining issues are small details, not structure.

Up from 7.2 (second review) and 6.4 (first). Fixed since: list text size, LINE in the footers, and the cramped single-column Jeans slide.

7.4/10Average of five scores (third review). Reviewed: 7 slides at 1080 × 1350, from projects/stock-drop/output/2026-10/.

Direction

8/10Strong

One direction carries through all 8 slides: navy, gold and warm cream, Cormorant Garamond headings, soft rounded containers, and pills used for the same job on the cover (sections) and in the footer (WhatsApp). The gold WhatsApp pill on the cover and the navy one on the list slides are a deliberate pair. One drift remains: the cover’s logo plate is the only element with a hard drop shadow and a framed rule.

  • Keep One shape language: pills and soft containers.
  • Fix Use the light traced SVG on the cover instead of the shadowed plate.

Hierarchy

8/10Strong

Clear tiers on every list slide: the section name (62px serif), then the bale name (32px) with its packing in gold, then the bale-count box. The WhatsApp pill is the strongest element in each footer, which is right for the one call to action. One gap on slide 05 (Jeans, two columns): the single “BALES” label sits only above the right column, so the left column’s boxes have no label.

  • Keep Name → packing → bale-count box.
  • Fix On two-column slides, label both columns of boxes.

Detail

7/10Strong

Clean execution: section names line up with the text inside the containers (22px inset), every bale-count box is the same width with centred figures, and lining numerals hold everywhere. An expert eye still finds misses. The cover’s drawing sits about 45px lower than the logo plate’s top edge. On slide 05 the two wrapped names make three lines of text in a 96px row, so those rows feel crowded. Slide 08 ends with about a quarter of the slide empty. “Carhartt jeans” (lowercase) is the first line on slide 05.

  • Keep Equal box widths; inset section names.
  • Fix Align the drawing with the logo plate.

Function

7/10Strong

It does its job well on a phone now. Bale names went from 24px to 32px (28px on the Jeans slide), packing from 17px to 20px, and WhatsApp is the only contact, in a large pill on every slide. Every section is whole on one slide, and the cover lists all 17 sections. Still short of the social skill’s ~28pt (about 37px) guideline for body text, though: 32px at 1080 wide is about 11.5pt on a phone.

  • Keep WhatsApp pill on every slide.
  • Quick win Try 34–36px bale names on one-column slides; most names have room.

Originality

7/10Strong

The gold line drawing of strapped bales is still the memorable move, and it shows the product itself. The separate gold bale-count boxes and the two-column Jeans slide make it read more like a catalogue than the usual flat stock graphic. The rest is suitably restrained.

  • Keep The bale drawing.
