Critique · 5-dimension review

Stock Drop, October 2026

The direction is clear and consistent. One collision on the cover and small text on the list slides keep it short of premium.

6.4/10Average of five scores. Reviewed: 7 slides at 1080 × 1350, from projects/stock-drop/output/2026-10/.

Direction

8/10Strong

One direction throughout. The navy, gold and off-white palette comes straight from the logo, with Cormorant Garamond for headings and Inter for data, and every list slide (02–07) uses the same template. One drift: the footer logo (.footer .logo, 72px) reads as a small pasted tile, while the cover gives the logo a framed plate with a gold rule.

  • Keep The single list template.
  • Fix Give the footer logo the cover plate’s treatment, or remove it.

Hierarchy

7/10Strong

Clear tiers on every list slide: the section name (54px serif) first, then the bale line (24px Inter), then the quantity at the right edge. Two things compete. The grey “BALES” label appears 17 times across 6 slides, once per section, so slide 03 has five identical labels. And the bold gold packing tags (“200 PCS”) pull the eye almost as much as the descriptions.

  • Keep Section name → line → quantity.
  • Fix Show “BALES” once per slide, or drop it.

Detail

6/10Functional

Mostly clean: leaders and right-aligned figures line up, and lining numerals fixed the dropped 3. Two visible misses. On the cover, the descender of the “p” in “Drop” (200px, line-height 0.86) runs into “October 2026”, which sits only 18px below (.cover-text .month). The footer wordmark inside the 72px logo is about 11px tall, so it’s unreadable. Slide 07 also leaves the lower 60% empty.

  • Keep Leader and figure alignment.
  • Fix Widen the gap under “Drop” so the “p” clears the date.

Function

6/10Functional

It does its main job: every section is on one slide, and WhatsApp, LINE and Instagram are on every slide. But at --row: 54px the bale lines render at about 24px on a 1080px slide, roughly 8.5pt on a phone and under the social skill’s ~28pt minimum. Packing (17px) and “BALES” (14px) are smaller still. The cover also doesn’t say what’s inside, so as a thumbnail it gives buyers no reason to swipe.

  • Keep Contacts in every footer.
  • Fix Decide on text size: 7 slides at 24px, or 8–9 slides with bigger text.

Originality

5/10Functional

A catalogue or menu-style table with dotted leaders and serif section names. It suits a premium wholesaler and reads more professional than the usual flat bale graphics, but nothing in it is unexpected. For a monthly stock list, that restraint is the right call.

  • Keep The restraint.
  • Quick win Optional: name the biggest sections under the cover title.
