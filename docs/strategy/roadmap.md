# Roadmap

Recommended next steps, in priority order.

## 1. Trust basics (this week, near-zero cost)

- [ ] Convert Facebook to a proper business Page with the category "Wholesale & Supply Store"
- [ ] Use a clean logo without the sticker, and the container-loading photo as the cover
- [ ] Add WhatsApp, LINE, address, and hours everywhere
- [ ] Ask past buyers for Google reviews

## 2. Weekly stock list

- [ ] Design a branded "Ready to Ship" bale list graphic (category, piece count, optional "from" price)
- [ ] Post it weekly on IG, FB, and WhatsApp broadcast. This is the core sales tool in the niche

Store each list in `content/stock-lists/`.

## 3. Website

The biggest single upgrade, and likely Hammad's deliverable. See the [website scope](../website/scope.md).

## 4. Split content by audience

- **Thai reels for local resellers:** warehouse visits, sourcing stories
- **English or subtitled versions for export buyers:** bale openings, container loading, shipment proof

## 5. Launch TikTok

Repost the same reels. TikTok is where ZewGrags gets its reach (see [competitors](../research/competitors.md)).

## 6. Constant proof content

Container loading, buyer unboxings, testimonials, and the Google rating on screen.

## 7. Resolve the logo and trademark risk

Do this before spending more on the brand (see [business profile](../research/business-profile.md#brand)).
