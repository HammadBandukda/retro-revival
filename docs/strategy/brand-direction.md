# Brand Direction

Supplied by the client in the Stock Drop brief (Oct 2026). Applies to every project.

## Positioning

One of the most professional vintage clothing wholesalers in Southeast Asia, **not a budget warehouse**. The look should be closer to a premium fashion catalogue than an inventory sheet.

**References for feel:** Ralph Lauren, Carhartt WIP, END Clothing, Aimé Leon Dore, The RealReal, luxury editorial catalogues.

## Personality

Premium · Trustworthy · Professional · Modern · International · Organized · Reliable · Authentic

A first-time visitor should think:

- "This warehouse looks professional."
- "I can trust this supplier."
- "This is a business I would like to buy from."

## Audience

Vintage wholesalers, vintage boutique owners, Instagram live sellers, online resellers, export buyers, importers, and container buyers.

**Primary markets:** Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Philippines, Brunei, Australia.
**Secondary:** wholesale buyers worldwide.

> These are *target* markets. They are not confirmed shipping destinations, so don't present them as "countries we ship to" (see [open questions](../client/open-questions.md)).

## Typography

- **Cormorant Garamond** for headings and numbers: the editorial, heritage voice.
- **Inter** for body text, labels, and data: clean and very readable on phones.
