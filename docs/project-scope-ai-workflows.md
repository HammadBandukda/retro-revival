# Retro Revival Thailand
## Project scope & scalable AI workflow
**Working brief | 13 October 2026**

### Business & goal
Retro Revival Co. Ltd. is a B2B vintage clothing wholesaler at Warehouse C5, Lat Krabang, Bangkok. It sells sealed, pre-sorted bales to resellers. We want Thai and international buyers to understand the stock, trust the business and enquire through WhatsApp or Instagram.

### Confirmed scope: the Stock Drop
Build a reusable inventory-to-content process, starting with October 2026. Deliver eight mobile-friendly Instagram slides (1080 × 1350), a carousel preview, a ZIP of images and a line-by-line inventory check. Match the July visual style: real warehouse photography, the official logo, near-black, gold and cream, bold headings and readable tables. Preserve all 80 inventory rows, 17 sections and 641 bales; only approved spelling and section-name corrections apply.

### How the AI workflow is being set up
**One shared foundation in GitHub:** business facts, brand assets, decisions and approved inventory live in the Retro Revival repository. ChatGPT and Claude use these same sources, with separate workspaces for prompts, generated outputs and documentation.

**Repeatable production:** approved inventory → validation → slide plan → design and rendering → accuracy and mobile-readability checks → owner review → publishing → results review. Generative imagery supports creative elements; stock names, quantities and packing are rendered from structured data. The official logo and real warehouse photographs stay intact.

**Human accountability:** the business owner confirms arrivals, availability, pricing and commercial claims; AI prepares designs, copy and checks. Changes and approvals are recorded so the next session can resume without reconstructing context.

### How we make it scalable
- **Reuse the system:** each new drop changes the inventory and date while using the same brand templates, corrections and checks.
- **Keep outputs traceable:** date each release, retain source data and prompts, and record checks, approvals and final exports.
- **Build once, adapt by channel:** extend approved content into English/Thai captions, WhatsApp stock lists and social formats.
- **Add automation in stages:** first automate validation and exports; then scheduling and measurement once connected account access is verified.
- **Measure business outcomes:** track qualified enquiries, warehouse visits and orders alongside content performance. Use a drop identifier to connect enquiries with each release.
