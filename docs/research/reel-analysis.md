# Reel Analysis: Warehouse Location Video

A 42-second reel spoken in **Thai** and aimed at **local Thai resellers**.

## Transcript

Machine-transcribed from Thai and translated. It covers the first ~16 seconds; the rest is mostly music.

> "Hey sellers and friends, ever wondered where second-hand clothing sellers get their stock from? Today I'll take you to see. This is our warehouse, on Krungthep Kreetha New Cut Road, just 20 minutes from Suvarnabhumi Airport. Just search 'Retro Revival' [on Google Maps]…"

## Structure

1. Hook outside the warehouse, presenter to camera
2. Quick cut to the bale stacks
3. Thai text overlays: the road name, then "20 min from Suvarnabhumi"
4. Screen recording of a Google Maps search for "Retro Revival Co. Ltd." (rated listing)
5. Dashcam drive-up
6. Walk into the warehouse: bale walls, branded banner, presenter hype
7. End card: address, WhatsApp, LINE ID

## Assessment

**Strengths:**

- A strong, relatable hook ("where do sellers source?")
- Real proof of location
- A clear call-to-action card

**Gaps:**

- No English subtitles
- No prices, minimum order, or shipping info
- The Google rating, which is the best proof, is on screen for only about 2 seconds

## Insight: two audiences, one feed

1. **Local Thai resellers** (IG and TikTok sellers, market stalls): Thai-language content, walk-in visits
2. **International wholesale buyers**: English-language content, export, container shipping

This split drives the content plan in the [roadmap](../strategy/roadmap.md) and the bilingual requirement in the [website scope](../../projects/website/scope.md).
