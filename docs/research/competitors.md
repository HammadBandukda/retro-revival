## Industry pattern

A significant share of Thailand's vintage-bale export trade appears to be **Pakistan-connected**: the Wing999 founder, the SPS phone number, and Retro Revival's likely owner. Hammad is Karachi-based and speaks Urdu, which may help with communication and business development.

## What the leaders have that Retro Revival lacks

- Visible proof: reviews, follower counts, shipment content
- A website
- Transparent pricing, or at least a catalog
- A presence on several channels, especially TikTok
- A recurring stock list
